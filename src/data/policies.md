# Our Policies

### Refund & Cancellation Policy
* **Deposits:** Non-refundable once the date and time are locked. No exceptions are made, including COVID-19 situations, rain, or shine. 
* **Changes:** No rescheduling of dates, times, or addresses.
* **Cancellation Fee:** A fee of **$200 per gaming trailer/bus and per service** (including jumpers, activity, balloon décor, flower décor, nerf battle, popcorn machine, etc.) applies per day if canceled.
* **Payment:** A valid credit or debit card is required to guarantee and pay for your reservation.

---

### Additional Policies (Schools, Churches, Fairs, Community Events, Tournaments, Businesses & Corporations)
* **Deposits:** Non-refundable.
* **Cancellation Fee:** $200 per gaming trailer or gaming bus if canceled by a representative or client.
* **Rescheduling:** No rescheduling permitted (no exceptions).

---

### Damage & Injury
* **Property Damage:** Ultimate Players and its employees are not responsible for any property damage (above ground or below ground) resulting from client requests to position the vehicle in a specific location.
* **Equipment Damage:** Ultimate Players reserves the right to charge the client's credit card for any damage to the trailer or equipment caused by careless or willful behavior from guests.
* **Injuries:** Ultimate Players assumes no responsibility for injuries sustained on the client’s property or designated location. All liability rests solely on the client/host.

---

### Supervised Children
* **Adult Presence:** A parent or responsible adult must be present inside or just outside the trailer at all times.
* **Children 5 & Under:** Must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at all times inside the gaming trailer or bus.

---

### Bad Weather
* **Design:** Trailers are climate-controlled (heating and A/C) and insulated for all weather conditions.
* **Severe Weather:** In cases of severe weather (lightning, high winds, flooding, extremely heavy rain, snow, or ice), Ultimate Players reserves the right to reschedule to guarantee safety.

---

### Food, Drinks, Alcohol & Drugs
* **Food & Drinks:** Strictly prohibited inside the video game trailer. Please consume all food, candy, and drinks outside prior to entering.
* **Alcohol & Drugs:** Not allowed or tolerated in or around the gaming trailer/equipment. Ultimate Players reserves the right to refuse, cancel, or cut short any event if alcohol or drugs are present or distributed.

---

### Privacy Policy
* **Data Usage:** Information collected on the site is used solely to facilitate bookings and enhance customer experience.
* **Data Sharing:** We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

---

### Pictures & Video
* **Media Capture:** A game coach will take photos/videos during the party from inside and outside the trailer.
* **Social Media:** Photos may be posted to Facebook or Instagram, where you can view and save them.
* **Opt-Out:** If you do not wish to have photos/videos taken or posted, you must notify a representative at the time of booking.

---

### Truck, Trailer & Parking
* **Parking Location:** We attempt to park in the most flat, level, and logical spot (usually directly in front of the residence). We may need to block your driveway.
* **Coach Discretion:** The game coach has final authority on parking. They will not park anywhere that poses a danger to persons, property, or equipment.
* **Truck Attachment:** The truck will never be disconnected from the trailer during an event.
* **Restricted Areas:** Ultimate Players **does not** park in driveways, alleys, backyards, or residential parking structures.

---

### Birthday Parties & Events
* **Arrival:** We arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to set up.
* **Space Requirements:** Clients must ensure enough space is clear to park the truck and trailer (typically the length of 4 cars).
* **Time Policy:** Party time will not be extended to compensate for time lost clearing parking spots or moving cars.

---

### Special Requests (Extra Costs Apply)
The following requests incur additional fees:
* Parking in a driveway or inside private property.
* Parking inside a business or business parking structure.
* Parking inside a park.
* Specific or extra game requests.
* Certificate of Insurance (COI) requests.
* Online games setup.
